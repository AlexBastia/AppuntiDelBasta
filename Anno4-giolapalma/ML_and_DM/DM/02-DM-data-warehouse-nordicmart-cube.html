About this data
  • Grain: one row = one product, in one transaction, at one store, on one day — the exact grain from the “single most important decision” slide.
  • Dimensions: Time (Day → Month → Quarter → Year), Product (SKU → Category → Department), Store (Store → Region), plus Customer Segment as a filter-only attribute.
  • In this synthetic dataset each city has exactly one store, so Store and City coincide — the full hierarchy from Lecture 2 is Store → City → Region.
  • 560 fact rows, 2026 only. Electronics and Apparel revenue by quarter is calibrated to match the Lecture 2 slide exactly; every other figure is illustrative.
  • Measures (Revenue, Quantity) are additive — they roll up by summing, consistent with the “Facts and measures” slide.

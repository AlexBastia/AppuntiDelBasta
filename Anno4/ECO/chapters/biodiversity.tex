Levels of biodiversity:
\begin{itemize}
\item Species diversity
\item Genetic diversity
\item Ecological diversity
\end{itemize}

How can we measusre biodiversity?
\begin{itemize}
\item Species richness: n of species
\item Abundance: n of individuals
\item Shannon diversity index: distribution of abundance across species
\end{itemize}
This can be done locally, regionally or ... ($ \alpha, \beta, \gamma $).
